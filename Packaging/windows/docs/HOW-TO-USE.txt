 How to use Vocalise-Rider

The full illustrated guide is Vocalise-Rider Guide.html (same folder). Open it in any web browser. Inside the plugin, press i to open it, or ? for an overlay that explains each control on the screen.

 In 30 seconds
1. Put Vocalise-Rider on the vocal track.
2. Play the vocal for 10+ seconds. Grey = original, green = after riding.
3. Drag the purple (Tame Peaks) and blue (Lower Levels) lines until the green is flatter than the grey.
4. Leave OUTPUT at 0.0 dB (unity).

 What the lines mean
- TAME PEAKS +4.0 dB: the ceiling sits 4 dB louder than the Target. Louder parts are pulled down to it.
- LOWER LEVELS -7.0 dB: the floor sits 7 dB quieter than the Target. Quieter parts are lifted up to it.
- TARGET: the loudness you aim for. SILENCE: below this is breath or noise and is left alone.

The numbers table shows real levels in dB, before (grey) and after (green). A smaller GAP after riding means a more even vocal.

 Controls
SPEED, TARGET and SILENCE knobs; LIVE / WRITE / READ for recording the ride as automation; SMART TARGET, SMART SILENCE and PROTECT "S" SOUNDS switches. The cog opens Advanced: Lookahead, Leveling, exact line amounts and AUTO SET.
